Privacy notice

Your enquiry stays between you and the services you choose.

Effective 29 August 2026. This notice explains the limited information involved when you browse the Emiral page or choose to call or use WhatsApp.

Site measurement

Measurement is off by default. If you select “Allow measurement,” the site may send the event name phone_order_start or whatsapp_order_start, the contact method and the campaign reference EM-NG-SEARCH to the configured Google tag.

Product names, message text, phone numbers and health-related information are not included in analytics event parameters. You can change your choice from “Measurement settings” in the Emiral page footer.

Calls and WhatsApp

Calling uses your phone provider. WhatsApp links open a prefilled order enquiry in WhatsApp, but you choose whether to send it. WhatsApp then handles the message under its own privacy terms.

Please share only the information needed to arrange the order. Do not send medical records or other sensitive health information.

Order records

For confirmed orders, FemmeEssencia may keep the information needed to fulfil and account for the order, such as the ordered item, destination, amount, payment status and delivery or return status.

The internal advertising log is designed to record campaign reference, product, revenue, costs and order status without customer names, phone numbers or medical information.

Your choices

  • Use essential-only browsing without optional measurement.
  • Ask what order information is held about you or request a correction.
  • Choose not to send a prefilled WhatsApp message.
