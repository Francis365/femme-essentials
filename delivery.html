Delivery, payment & returns

Review the complete order before you pay.

Effective 29 August 2026. Stock, current price, delivery cost and timing are confirmed for each order because these details can change.

Availability and price

Items shown on the Emiral page are part of the catalogue, not a promise of current stock. FemmeEssencia will confirm the exact item, pack details and selling price before requesting payment.

Do not send payment until you have received and accepted the complete order summary.

Delivery

Orders are handled from 4A Ogunusi Road, Ojodu Berger, Lagos. Delivery is available to Nigerian destinations subject to confirmation.

The delivery fee and estimated timing depend on the destination and available delivery service. Both are stated in the order summary before payment. International delivery is not offered through the advertising page.

Payment

The accepted payment method and payment instructions are supplied with the final order summary. Verify that the product price, delivery fee and total match what was agreed before paying.

Keep your payment record and the written order summary until the order is complete.

Wrong or damaged items

Check the delivered item as soon as practical. If the item is different from the confirmed order or arrives damaged, contact FemmeEssencia promptly with the order details and clear photographs.

A replacement, return or refund is assessed from the confirmed order and the condition of the item. Do not return an item without first receiving return instructions.

Before ordering

This shop information does not provide medical advice. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns. Product suitability for an individual is not guaranteed by an order enquiry.
